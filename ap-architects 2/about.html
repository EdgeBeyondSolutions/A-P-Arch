About

A small studio, deliberately.

Since 1998

Founded in 1998 by Alvin G. Peters, AP Architects has built a diverse portfolio of commercial and residential projects across San Antonio and the Hill Country. We work as collaborative teams that stay with our projects through occupancy — because a building isn't finished when the drawings are stamped, it's finished when the people using it are settled in.

We're privileged to work with our clients and their communities, and we take seriously our responsibility to the public trust, to diverse stakeholders, to the environment, and to each other. Our small size is a choice: it's what lets us give every project the care and attention it needs.
